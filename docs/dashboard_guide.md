# Dashboard guide

## Audience

Operations, business intelligence, risk, and compliance stakeholders who need a high-level view of complaint workload and response performance.

## Views

### 1. Monthly Consumer Complaints, 2020–2025

Use this line chart to identify changes in complaint workload and potential periods for further investigation.

### 2. Complaint Volume by Financial Product

Use the ranked bar chart to identify product categories that account for the greatest share of recorded complaints.

### 3. Top 10 Consumer Complaint Issues

Use this chart to understand which reported issues appear most frequently in the selected period.

### 4. Timely Response Rate by Product

Use the dot plot to compare the share of complaints marked as receiving a timely response across product groups.

## Design choices

- A 2020–2025 scope keeps the dashboard focused on recent operational patterns.
- Ranking charts make product and issue concentration easy to scan.
- The response-rate chart uses a percentage scale so differences are visible across products.
- The dashboard includes a source and scope footer so viewers can assess the analysis context.
